title = "Tame sums commute with limits"
tags = ["tame-sequences"]

created = 2026-11-05

Let $a^{(k)}$ be tame sequences (\ref{A2DF}) that converge termwise to $a$, with $|a^{(k)}_n| \le c_n$ for a tame sequence $c$. Then
\[
  \sum_{n \in \N} a^{(k)}_n \to \sum_{n \in \N} a_n \quad \text{as } k \to \infty.
\]
