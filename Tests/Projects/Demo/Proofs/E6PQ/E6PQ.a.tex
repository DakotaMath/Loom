Let $|b_n| \le M$ for every $n$. Then $\sum_n |a_n b_n| \le M \sum_n |a_n| < \infty$: this is the case $p = 1$ of \paperref{7RT2}. With $b = a$, which is bounded by \ref{D5MN}, it shows that $a^2$ is tame too.
