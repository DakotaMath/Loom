Write $M = \sup_{n \in \N} |b_n|$, which is finite because $b$ is bounded. For every $N$,
\[
  \sum_{n < N} |a_n b_n| \le \sum_{n < N} M |a_n| \le M \sum_{n \in \N} |a_n|.
\]
The partial sums on the left increase and are bounded, so they converge, and $ab$ is tame. This is the case $p = 1$ of \paperref{7RT2}. Applied with $b = a$, which is bounded by \ref{D5MN}, it shows that $a^2$ is tame too.
