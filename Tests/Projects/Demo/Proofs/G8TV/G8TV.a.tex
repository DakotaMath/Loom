Let $\varepsilon > 0$. By \ref{C4JK} applied to $c$ there is $N$ with $T_N(c) < \varepsilon$. The finitely many terms below $N$ converge, and the terms from $N$ on contribute at most $2 \varepsilon$ to the difference of the sums, for every $k$.
