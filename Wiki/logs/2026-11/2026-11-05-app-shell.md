## Learned
- QLockFile treats a lock older than 30 s as stale even while its owner runs; its host line on Windows is the capitalised computer name ([gotcha](../../gotchas/qt/qlockfile-stale-by-age.md)).
- A callback named `emit` compiles to nothing: Qt's empty macro ([gotcha](../../gotchas/qt/emit-is-a-macro.md)).
- On this machine `LoomAppTests.exe` writes nothing to a console or pipe (offscreen platform too); `-o <file>,txt` works. Not investigated; noted in the PR.
- Screenshots caught two layout bugs the tests couldn't: an item's text drawn under its row widget, and frames around empty status-bar items.
